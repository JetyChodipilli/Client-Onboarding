# Foundation Page Override

The generated master direction remains authoritative for semantic palette, accessible contrast, spacing, responsiveness and restrained glass surfaces. This page intentionally overrides two recommendations:

- Typography uses locally packaged Geist instead of a Google Fonts request so production builds do not depend on a third-party font network.
- Motion remains subtle: one hero entrance, one progress-line reveal and grouped section reveals. All motion renders directly in its final state under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

The page is an architecture/status foundation, not a production dashboard and not a business workflow screen.
